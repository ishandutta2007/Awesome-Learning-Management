# Awesome-Learning-Management

## Top Learning Management System (LMS) Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Corporate Training, Online Courses, Employee Learning, Compliance Training & Educational Delivery*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Learning Management Systems (LMS)**. These platforms deliver, track, and manage training content for employees, customers, students, and partners—covering course authoring, enrollments, assessments, reporting, and certifications.



**Examples** include Docebo, TalentLMS, MoodleCloud, Absorb LMS, LearnUpon, Thinkific, Teachable, Kajabi, Litmos, and Canvas LMS (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: LMS is one of the strongest open-source categories. **Moodle**, **Open edX**, **Canvas LMS** (open core), **Chamilo**, and related projects power millions of learners worldwide. This section is heavily expanded.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Docebo](https://www.docebo.com/)**  

  Enterprise learning platform with AI-driven personalization, strong customization, and broad integration options for large workforces.



- **[TalentLMS](https://www.talentlms.com/)**  

  User-friendly LMS popular with small and mid-sized teams for fast deployment, gamification, and affordable scaling.



- **[MoodleCloud](https://moodle.com/)**  

  Official hosted Moodle service providing the open-source LMS as a managed cloud offering without self-hosting overhead.



- **[Absorb LMS](https://www.absorblms.com/)**  

  Corporate LMS focused on employee, partner, and customer training with strong reporting and e-commerce capabilities.



- **[LearnUpon](https://www.learnupon.com/)**  

  Structured, scalable LMS designed for organizations training employees, customers, and partners with solid support and administration.



- **[Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com/)**  

  Creator- and business-oriented platform for building, marketing, and selling online courses.



- **[Teachable](https://teachable.com/)**  

  Course platform aimed at entrepreneurs and educators who want to create and sell digital learning products.



- **[Kajabi](https://kajabi.com/)**  

  All-in-one knowledge-commerce platform combining courses, marketing, and sales tools for online educators and creators.



- **[Litmos (SAP)](https://www.litmos.com/)**  

  Corporate training LMS often used for compliance, onboarding, and employee development at scale.



- **[Canvas LMS (Instructure)](https://www.instructure.com/canvas)**  

  Widely adopted educational LMS (K-12 and higher ed) with a modern UX; also available as open-source for self-hosting.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[Moodle](https://github.com/moodle/moodle)**  

  The most widely deployed open-source LMS in the world—highly extensible via plugins, used across education and corporate training.



- **[Open edX](https://github.com/openedx)**  

  Open-source platform behind edX.org, built for large-scale online courses, MOOCs, and institutional learning programs.



- **[Canvas LMS (open source)](https://github.com/instructure/canvas-lms)**  

  AGPL open-source edition of Canvas maintained by Instructure—modern LMS suitable for self-hosted higher-ed and training deployments.



- **[Chamilo](https://github.com/chamilo/chamilo-lms)**  

  Open-source LMS focused on ease of use and accessibility, used by millions of learners worldwide.



- **[Sakai](https://github.com/sakaiproject)**  

  Open-source collaboration and learning platform historically strong in higher education and research institutions.



- **[ILIAS](https://github.com/ILIAS-eLearning)**  

  Mature open-source LMS with a strong presence in European higher education and public-sector training.



- **[OpenOLAT](https://github.com/OpenOLAT)**  

  Open-source LMS oriented toward higher education and blended learning scenarios.



- **[Forma LMS](https://github.com/)**  

  Open-source LMS derived from earlier corporate learning platforms, aimed at enterprise training use cases.



- **[Totara / Moodle Workplace–style open extensions](https://github.com/)**  

  Open and commercial extensions that add multi-tenancy, talent, and compliance features on top of Moodle.



- **[LTI and learning-tool open integrations](https://github.com/)**  

  Open LTI tool providers and consumers that extend any LMS with external content, assessments, and analytics.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Deploying **Moodle** for maximum plugin ecosystem and global community support.

- Choosing **Open edX** for MOOC-scale or highly customized institutional learning experiences.

- Self-hosting **Canvas LMS** when a modern UI and higher-ed feature set are required under AGPL.

- Using **Chamilo** for lighter, accessibility-focused deployments.

- Accepting that polished AI personalization, turnkey multi-tenancy, enterprise support, and creator e-commerce features still favor commercial platforms (Docebo, Absorb, LearnUpon, Thinkific, Teachable, Kajabi, etc.).

- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, customization, and cost control for education and internal training.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Self-host Moodle or Open edX → add plugins/XBlocks for specific needs → integrate SSO and HRIS via APIs → deliver content and track completions → report to compliance or talent systems. Suitable for universities, governments, and organizations with IT capacity. Many companies still choose commercial LMS platforms for faster time-to-value and reduced operations.
